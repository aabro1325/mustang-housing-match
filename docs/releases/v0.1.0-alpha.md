**Release:** `v0.1.0-alpha` (preview, for reference users)
**Download:** `mustang-housing-match-v0.1.0-alpha.zip`, attached to this GitHub release
**Repo:** https://github.com/aabro1325/mustang-housing-match
**Give feedback:** [FEEDBACK FORM LINK]

## What it is

Mustang Housing Match is a Chrome side-panel extension that helps Cal Poly students check off-campus rental listings against their own priorities and decide with their roommates. Open a listing on Zillow, Craigslist or Apartments.com, click **Analyze listing**, and the side panel shows a 0–100 Fit Score, an estimate of what you'll really pay each month, the commute to Cal Poly, and the questions to ask the landlord. You never have to leave the listing.

This is the first preview build. It's meant for trying out the core flow and telling us what works and what doesn't. Expect rough edges.

## Features in this alpha

- **Listing capture on supported sites.** The panel detects when you're on a supported listing page. **Analyze listing** reads the address, rent, bedrooms, bathrooms, lease length, amenities and fees from the page. It only reads the page when you click the button, never in the background.
- **"Not listed," never guessed.** Anything the listing doesn't say is labeled *Not listed*. Values the tool fills in (like utilities) are labeled *Estimate*.
- **Review and correct.** Before you see a score, you can fix any captured detail, pick a floor plan on pages that list several, and choose whether the rent is **per unit** (split among roommates) or **per person**.
- **Facebook posts and other sites.** On Facebook Marketplace or group posts, select the post text and click **Analyze selected text**, or paste the text into the panel. On any other page, use **Add listing manually**.
- **Personal preferences.** Set your maximum monthly budget, how you get to campus (walk, bike, bus, car), maximum commute time, lease length, room type, the amenities you want (parking, laundry, utilities included, pets, furnished), and how much Cost, Commute, Lease and Amenities each matter to you (Low, Med, High).
- **Fit Score (0–100).** The score is calculated the same way every time from the listing and your preferences, and it recalculates when you change a preference. "Why this score?" shows how many points each category earned.
- **Total cost, not just rent.** You get an estimated monthly total per person: base rent plus utilities, internet, parking and other monthly fees. The deposit and application fee are shown separately.
- **Commute to Cal Poly.** Distance and walk, bike, bus and car times to Kennedy Library (1 Grand Ave), counted in the Commute part of the score. See the limitations below for how this works in the alpha.
- **Benefits, watch-outs and landlord questions.** The panel lists why a place scored well, what to double-check, and questions generated from every *Not listed* or *Estimate* value.
- **Saved tab.** Save listings to a list, which is created for you the first time you save. Each saved listing has a status (Saved, Considering, Tour Scheduled, Applied, Rejected, Selected), a tour date and time, dated notes, landlord questions and a Finalist flag. Duplicate listings are caught, so saving the same place twice opens the one you already have.
- **Compare tab.** Compare two or more Finalists side by side: Fit Score, estimated monthly total, commute, lease, benefits and watch-outs.
- **Roommates tab.** Name a list, see its members, get an invitation link, check the Group Fit (the average of each member's Fit Score), and mark the group's choice.

## Supported sites

| Site | How it works |
|---|---|
| Zillow (`zillow.com/homedetails/...` home pages and `zillow.com/apartments/...` building pages) | Automatic capture |
| Craigslist (`*.craigslist.org` listing posts) | Automatic capture |

| Facebook Marketplace items and group posts | Select or paste the post text |
| Any other site (Redfin, Instagram, school housing boards, etc.) | Add listing manually |

## Install (about 2 minutes)

You need Google Chrome 116 or newer on a laptop or desktop. Chrome extensions don't run on phones.

1. Download `mustang-housing-match-v0.1.0-alpha.zip` from the Assets section of this release.
2. **Unzip it.** On Mac, double-click the zip. On Windows, right-click it and choose **Extract All**. You should get a folder that has `manifest.json` directly inside it.
3. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (the toggle in the top-right corner).
5. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the **unzipped folder** (the one with `manifest.json` inside it, not the .zip file).
6. "Mustang Housing Match 0.1.0-alpha" now appears in your list of extensions. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the Chrome toolbar and **pin** Mustang Housing Match so it's easy to find.

Keep the unzipped folder where it is. Chrome loads the extension from that folder, so deleting or moving it removes the extension.

## How to use it

1. **Open the panel.** Click the Mustang Housing Match icon in your toolbar. The side panel opens on the right.
2. **Set your preferences.** Click **Set my preferences**, fill in your budget, how you get to campus, max commute, lease, room and amenities, set how much each category matters, then click **Save preferences**. You can skip this and change it any time with **Edit preferences**.
3. **Open a listing.** In the same window, open a rental on Zillow, Craigslist or Apartments.com. The panel shows **Listing detected**.
4. **Analyze.** Click **Analyze listing**.
5. **Review.** Check the captured details and fix anything that's wrong or missing. Pick a floor plan if asked, and set **That rent is** to per unit or per person. Then continue.
6. **Read the scorecard.** You'll see the Fit Score, Why this score?, the estimated monthly total, the commute to Cal Poly, benefits and watch-outs, and the questions to ask the landlord.
7. **Save it.** Click **Save listing**. It goes to the **Saved** tab, where you can set a status, add notes or a tour time, and mark it as a Finalist.
8. **Compare.** Once you have two or more Finalists, open the **Compare** tab.
9. **Facebook or other sites.** On a Facebook post, highlight the post text and click **Analyze selected text**, or paste it into **Post text**. On any other site, click **Add listing manually**.

If a page changes after you install or reload the extension, refresh the listing tab before clicking Analyze.

## Known limitations in this alpha

- **Commute times are limited.** This build uses built-in sample commute data and doesn't call a live maps service, so for most real addresses the commute shows "Address could not be located" and the Commute category scores 0. Cost, lease, amenities, landlord questions, saving and comparing all work normally. Live walk, bike, bus and car times are coming in the next build.
- **Roommate sharing only works on your own computer.** The shared-list backend isn't turned on in this build. Lists, statuses, notes and tours are saved in your Chrome profile and work fully for you, but an invitation link won't sync the list to a roommate's computer yet. You're welcome to try the Roommates tab and tell us what you'd expect.
- **No automatic capture on Redfin or other sites yet.** Use **Add listing manually** there.
- **Listing sites change their pages often.** If capture gets something wrong, fix it on the Review screen and please let us know which site and listing it was.
- **Check the Rent basis toggle.** For manual or pasted listings, rent counts as per unit unless you switch it to per person.
- **Everything is an estimate.** Utilities, internet, deposit and application fee are estimates when the listing doesn't state them, and they're labeled that way. Confirm with the landlord.
- **Developer-mode install.** Chrome may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" reminder. Click **Cancel** or close it to keep the extension. Updates aren't automatic, so we'll send a new zip.
- Desktop Chrome only. Edge and Brave may work but haven't been tested.

## Privacy

The extension only reads a page when you click **Analyze listing** or **Analyze selected text**. Your preferences and lists are saved in your own Chrome profile. This alpha doesn't send your data to any server.
