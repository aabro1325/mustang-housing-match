## What it is

Mustang Housing Match is a Chrome side-panel extension that helps Cal Poly students check an off-campus rental against their own priorities. Open a listing on Zillow, Craigslist, or Apartments.com, click **Analyze listing**, and the side panel shows a 0–100 Fit Score plus the reasons behind it. You never have to leave the listing.

This preview includes two features: capturing a listing, and a personalized fit score. Commute times, the monthly cost breakdown, shared roommate lists, and tour/compare are not in this build. They are coming in later releases.

## Features in this alpha

- **Listing capture on supported sites.** The panel detects when you're on a supported listing page. **Analyze listing** reads the address, monthly rent, bedrooms, bathrooms, lease length, and listed amenities. It only reads the page when you click the button, never in the background.
- **"Not listed," never guessed.** Anything the listing doesn't say is labeled *Not listed*.
- **Review and correct.** Before you see a score, you can fix any captured detail and pick a floor plan on pages that list several. Corrected info is what the score uses. You can also say whether the rent is **per unit** (split among roommates) or **per person**.
- **Facebook posts and other sites.** On Facebook Marketplace or group posts, select the post text and click **Analyze selected text**, or paste the text into the panel. On any other page, use **Add listing manually**. Unsupported pages show a clear message and do not error.
- **Personal preferences.** Set your maximum monthly budget, how you get to campus (walk, bike, bus, or car), lease length, room type (private or shared), and the amenities you want (parking, laundry, utilities included, pets, furnished). Set how much Cost, Lease, and Amenities each matter (Low, Med, High).
- **Fit Score (0–100).** Every analyzed listing gets a score from your saved preferences. The same listing and the same preferences always produce the same score. Changing a preference recalculates it. **Why this score?** shows how many points Cost, Lease, and Amenities earned. **Benefits and watch-outs** call out the main reasons.

## Supported sites

| Site | How it works |
|---|---|
| Zillow (`zillow.com/homedetails/...` home pages and `zillow.com/apartments/...` building pages) | Automatic capture |
| Craigslist (`*.craigslist.org` listing posts) | Automatic capture |

## Install (about 2 minutes)

You need Google Chrome 116 or newer on a laptop or desktop. Chrome extensions don't run on phones.

**From the repo (no build):** use **Code > Download ZIP** or `git clone`. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and select the `extension-alpha` folder (the one with `manifest.json` directly inside it, not the repo root).

**From this release:** download `mustang-housing-match-v0.1.0-alpha.zip` from Assets and unzip it. You should get a folder that has `manifest.json` directly inside it. Then:

1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (the toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the **unzipped folder** (the one with `manifest.json` inside it, not the .zip file).
4. "Mustang Housing Match 0.1.0-alpha" now appears in your list of extensions. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the Chrome toolbar and **pin** Mustang Housing Match so it's easy to find.

Keep that folder where it is. Chrome loads the extension from that folder, so deleting or moving it removes the extension.

## How to use it

1. **Open the panel.** Click the Mustang Housing Match icon in your toolbar. The side panel opens on the right.
2. **Set your preferences.** Click **Set my preferences**, fill in your budget, how you get to campus, lease, room, and amenities, set how much each category matters, then click **Save preferences**. You can change them any time with **Edit preferences**.
3. **Open a listing.** In the same window, open a rental on Zillow, Craigslist, or Apartments.com. The panel shows **Listing detected**.
4. **Analyze.** Click **Analyze listing**.
5. **Review.** Check the captured details and fix anything that's wrong or missing. Pick a floor plan if asked, and set **That rent is** to per unit or per person. Then continue.
6. **Read the scorecard.** You'll see the Fit Score, why it landed there, and the benefits and watch-outs.
7. **Change your mind.** Open **Change preferences**, update something, and save. The score updates from the listing you already captured.
8. **Facebook or other sites.** On a Facebook post, highlight the post text and click **Analyze selected text**, or paste it into **Post text**. On any other site, click **Add listing manually**.

If a page changes after you install or reload the extension, refresh the listing tab before clicking Analyze.

## Known limitations in this alpha

- **Commute is not in this build.** You can record how you get to campus, and that choice is saved, but distance and travel times are not shown and Commute is not part of the score. A missing commute does not pull the score down. Commute times are coming in a later release.
- **No monthly cost breakdown.** The score still considers your budget, but this preview does not show the estimated monthly total, deposit, fees, or questions for the landlord. That breakdown is coming later.
- **No shared lists yet.** Saving a listing, roommate invites, and a shared list are not in this preview.
- **No tour or compare workflow yet.** Statuses, tour times, notes, finalists, and side-by-side compare are coming in a later release.
- **No automatic capture on Redfin or other sites yet.** Use **Add listing manually** there.
- **Listing sites change their pages often.** If capture gets something wrong, fix it on the Review screen and please let us know which site and listing it was.
- **Check the Rent basis toggle.** For manual or pasted listings, rent counts as per unit unless you switch it to per person.
- **Developer-mode install.** Chrome may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" reminder. Click **Cancel** or close it to keep the extension. Updates aren't automatic, so we'll send a new zip.
- Desktop Chrome only. Edge and Brave may work but haven't been tested.

## Privacy

The extension only reads a page when you click **Analyze listing** or **Analyze selected text**. Your preferences are saved in your own Chrome profile. This alpha doesn't send your data to any server.
