You're invited to a shared housing list

Mustang Housing Match is a Chrome side-panel extension. To join this list:

  1. Install the extension (unpacked, from your group's build) and click its toolbar icon.
  2. Open the Roommates tab and choose Join with a link.
  3. Paste the link below, enter your name, and you're in.

The link contains a private token. Share it only with your roommates. Independent student project; not affiliated with Cal Poly or any listing site.
